**Supplementary Figure SF7 | Frontier-collapse-metric landscape (eleven frontier reasoning models on the management benchmark).** **a**, Middle-tier concentration: percentage of each model's predictions falling into the *strong + fair* tiers, sorted descending. Five of eleven models concentrate ≥95% of their predictions in the middle two tiers. **b**, Tier-recall heatmap: per-tier recall (%) for each model, with rows ordered as in **a**. Models in the high-concentration / low-accuracy lower-right quadrant exhibit the dominant frontier collapse mode. **d**, Distribution-balance ranking: normalized Shannon entropy of the predicted-tier distribution per model (0% = single-tier output, 100% = uniform across the four tiers), sorted descending. Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 2.5 Pro alone produce predictably balanced distributions; Claude Opus 4.6, DeepSeek V3.2 and MiniMax M2.5 collapse to near-single-tier output. *n* = 120 articles per model.
